NovaGit installation rules

1. Install NovaGit only on a computer you are allowed to use.
2. Git operations can change repository history and remote repositories.
   Review commands such as merge, pull, push, reset, and delete before use.
3. Keep backups of important repositories before performing destructive work.
4. Do not share OAuth tokens, passwords, or private repository URLs.
5. By continuing, you agree to use NovaGit responsibly and comply with the
   licenses of Git, .NET, and any services you connect to.
